
# Gold Ingot

This recipe allows you to produce Gold Ingots by mixing Golden Sheets with Lava using Create's Mechanical Mixer.

## Recipe Details

| Input | Amount |
|---|---|
| Golden Sheet | 1 |
| Lava | 100 mB |

## Output

| Output | Amount |
|---|---|
| Gold Ingot | 1 |

## Processing Information

- **Machine:** Mechanical Mixer and Basin
- **Input:** 1x Golden Sheet
- **Fluid Input:** 100 mB Lava
- **Output:** 1x Gold Ingot
- **Processing Time:** 100 ticks (5 seconds)

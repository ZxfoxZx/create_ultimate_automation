
# Iron Ingot

This recipe allows you to produce Iron Ingots by mixing Iron Sheets with Lava using Create's Mechanical Mixer.

## Recipe Details

| Input | Amount |
|---|---|
| ![Iron Sheet](@create:iron_sheet) Iron Sheet | 1 |
| ![Lava](@minecraft:fluid/lava.gif) Lava | 100 mB |

## Output

| Output | Amount |
|---|---|
| ![Iron Ingot](@minecraft:item/iron_ingot) Iron Ingot | 1 |

## Processing Information

- **Machine:** Mechanical Mixer and Basin
- **Input:** 1x Iron Sheet
- **Fluid Input:** 100 mB Lava
- **Output:** 1x Iron Ingot
- **Processing Time:** 100 ticks (5 seconds)

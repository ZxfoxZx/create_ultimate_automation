# Copper Ingot

This recipe allows you to produce Copper Ingots by mixing Copper Sheets with Lava using Create's Mechanical Mixer.

## Recipe Details

| Input | Amount |
|---|---:|
| ![Copper Sheet](../../assets/create/item/copper_sheet.png) Copper Sheet | 1 |
| ![Animated Lava](../../assets/minecraft/fluid/lava.gif) Lava | 100 mB |

## Output

| Output | Amount |
|---|---:|
| ![Copper Ingot](../../assets/minecraft/item/copper_ingot.png) Copper Ingot | 1 |

## Processing Information

- **Machine:** Mechanical Mixer and Basin
- **Input:** 1x Copper Sheet
- **Fluid Input:** 100 mB Lava
- **Output:** 1x Copper Ingot
- **Processing Time:** 100 ticks (5 seconds)

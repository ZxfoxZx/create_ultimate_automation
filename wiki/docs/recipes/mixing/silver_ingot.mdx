
# Silver Ingot

This recipe allows you to produce Silver Ingots by mixing Silver Sheets with Lava using Create's Mechanical Mixer.

## Required Mod

- **[Create: More Additions](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/create-more-additions)**

This recipe is only available when Create: More Additions is installed.

## Recipe Details

| Input | Amount |
|---|---|
| Silver Sheet | 1 |
| Lava | 100 mB |

## Output

| Output | Amount |
|---|---|
| Silver Ingot | 1 |

## Processing Information

- **Machine:** Mechanical Mixer and Basin
- **Input:** 1x Silver Sheet
- **Fluid Input:** 100 mB Lava
- **Output:** 1x Silver Ingot
- **Processing Time:** 100 ticks (5 seconds)
- **Required Mod:** Create: More Additions


# Asurine

This recipe allows you to produce Asurine by mixing Ochrum, Veridium, and Dark Prismarine using Create's Mechanical Mixer.

## Recipe Details

| Input | Amount |
|---|---|
| Ochrum | 1 |
| Veridium | 1 |
| Dark Prismarine | 1 |

## Output

| Output | Amount |
|---|---|
| Asurine | 2 |

## Processing Information

- **Machine:** Mechanical Mixer and Basin
- **Input:** 1x Ochrum
- **Input:** 1x Veridium
- **Input:** 1x Dark Prismarine
- **Output:** 2x Asurine
- **Processing Time:** 100 ticks (5 seconds)

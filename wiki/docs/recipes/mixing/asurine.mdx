
# Asurine

This recipe allows you to produce Asurine by mixing Ochrum, Veridium, and Dark Prismarine using Create's Mechanical Mixer.

## Recipe Details

| Input | Amount |
|---|---|
| ![Ochrum](@create:ochrum){item} Ochrum | 1 |
| ![Veridium](@create:veridium){item} Veridium | 1 |
| ![Dark Prismarine](@minecraft:item/dark_prismarine){item} Dark Prismarine | 1 |

## Output

| Output | Amount |
|---|---|
| ![Asurine](@create:asurine){item} Asurine | 2 |

## Processing Method

![Mechanical Mixer](@create:item/mechanical_mixer){item}

This recipe is processed using Create's Mechanical Mixer.

Place the required ingredients and fluids into a Basin and use the Mechanical Mixer to combine them into the resulting material.


## Processing Information

- **Machine:** Mechanical Mixer and Basin
- **Input:** 1x Ochrum
- **Input:** 1x Veridium
- **Input:** 1x Dark Prismarine
- **Output:** 2x Asurine
- **Processing Time:** 100 ticks (5 seconds)


# Aluminum Ingot

This recipe allows you to produce Aluminum Ingots by mixing Aluminum Sheets with Lava using Create's Mechanical Mixer.

## Required Mod

- **[Create: The Factory Must Grow (TFMG)](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/create-the-factory-must-grow)**

This recipe is only available when TFMG is installed.

## Recipe Details

| Input | Amount |
|---|---|
| ![Aluminum Sheet](@tfmg:aluminum_sheet){item} Aluminum Sheet | 1 |
| ![Lava](@create_ultimate_automation:fluid/lava.gif){item} Lava | 100 mB |

## Output

| Output | Amount |
|---|---|
| ![Aluminum Ingot](@tfmg:aluminum_ingot){item} Aluminum Ingot | 1 |

## Processing Method

![Mechanical Mixer](@create:item/mechanical_mixer){item}

This recipe is processed using Create's Mechanical Mixer.

Place the required ingredients and fluids into a Basin and use the Mechanical Mixer to combine them into the resulting material.


## Processing Information

- **Machine:** Mechanical Mixer and Basin
- **Input:** 1x Aluminum Sheet
- **Fluid Input:** 100 mB Lava
- **Output:** 1x Aluminum Ingot
- **Processing Time:** 100 ticks (5 seconds)
- **Required Mod:** Create: The Factory Must Grow

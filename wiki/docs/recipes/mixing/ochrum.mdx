
# Ochrum

This recipe allows you to produce Ochrum by mixing Sandstone, Limestone, and Gold Nuggets using Create's Mechanical Mixer.

## Recipe Details

| Input | Amount |
|---|---|
| ![Sandstone](@minecraft:item/sandstone) Sandstone | 1 |
| ![Limestone](@create:item/limestone) Limestone | 1 |
| ![Gold Nugget](@minecraft:item/gold_nugget) Gold Nugget | 1 |

## Output

| Output | Amount |
|---|---|
| ![Ochrum](@create:item/ochrum) Ochrum | 1 |

## Processing Information

- **Machine:** Mechanical Mixer and Basin
- **Input:** 1x Sandstone
- **Input:** 1x Limestone
- **Input:** 1x Gold Nugget
- **Output:** 1x Ochrum
- **Processing Time:** 100 ticks (5 seconds)

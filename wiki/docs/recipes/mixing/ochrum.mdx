
# Ochrum

This recipe allows you to produce Ochrum by mixing Sandstone, Limestone, and Gold Nuggets using Create's Mechanical Mixer.

## Recipe Details

| Input | Amount |
|---|---|
| ![Sandstone](@minecraft:item/sandstone){item} Sandstone | 1 |
| ![Limestone](@create:limestone){item} Limestone | 1 |
| ![Gold Nugget](@minecraft:item/gold_nugget){item} Gold Nugget | 1 |

## Output

| Output | Amount |
|---|---|
| ![Ochrum](@create:ochrum){item} Ochrum | 1 |

## Processing Method

![Mechanical Mixer](@create:mechanical_mixer){item}

This recipe is processed using Create's Mechanical Mixer.

Place the required ingredients and fluids into a Basin and use the Mechanical Mixer to combine them into the resulting material.

## Processing Information

- **Machine:** Mechanical Mixer and Basin
- **Input:** 1x Sandstone
- **Input:** 1x Limestone
- **Input:** 1x Gold Nugget
- **Output:** 1x Ochrum
- **Processing Time:** 100 ticks (5 seconds)

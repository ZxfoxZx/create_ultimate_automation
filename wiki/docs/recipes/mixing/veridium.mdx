
# Veridium

This recipe allows you to produce Veridium by mixing Prismarine Shards, Crimsite, and Quartz using Create's Mechanical Mixer.

## Recipe Details

| Input | Amount |
|---|---|
| Prismarine Shard | 1 |
| Crimsite | 1 |
| Quartz | 1 |

## Output

| Output | Amount |
|---|---|
| Veridium | 1 |

## Processing Information

- **Machine:** Mechanical Mixer and Basin
- **Input:** 1x Prismarine Shard
- **Input:** 1x Crimsite
- **Input:** 1x Quartz
- **Output:** 1x Veridium
- **Processing Time:** 100 ticks (5 seconds)


# Veridium

This recipe allows you to produce Veridium by mixing Prismarine Shards, Crimsite, and Quartz using Create's Mechanical Mixer.

## Recipe Details

| Input | Amount |
|---|---|
| ![Prismarine Shard](@minecraft:item/prismarine_shard){item} Prismarine Shard | 1 |
| ![Crimsite](@create:crimsite){item} Crimsite | 1 |
| ![Quartz](@minecraft:item/quartz){item} Quartz | 1 |

## Output

| Output | Amount |
|---|---|
| ![Veridium](@create:veridium){item} Veridium | 1 |

## Processing Method

![Mechanical Mixer](@create:mechanical_mixer){item}

This recipe is processed using Create's Mechanical Mixer.

Place the required ingredients and fluids into a Basin and use the Mechanical Mixer to combine them into the resulting material.

## Processing Information

- **Machine:** Mechanical Mixer and Basin
- **Input:** 1x Prismarine Shard
- **Input:** 1x Crimsite
- **Input:** 1x Quartz
- **Output:** 1x Veridium
- **Processing Time:** 100 ticks (5 seconds)

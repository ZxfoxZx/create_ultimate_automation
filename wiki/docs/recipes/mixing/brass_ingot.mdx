
# Brass Ingot

This recipe allows you to produce Brass Ingots by mixing Brass Sheets with Lava using Create's Mechanical Mixer.

## Recipe Details

| Input | Amount |
|---|---|
| ![Brass Sheet](@create:brass_sheet){item} Brass Sheet | 1 |
| ![Lava](@create_ultimate_automation:fluid/lava.gif){item} Lava | 100 mB |

## Output

| Output | Amount |
|---|---|
| ![Brass Ingot](@create:brass_ingot){item} Brass Ingot | 1 |

## Processing Information

- **Machine:** Mechanical Mixer and Basin
- **Input:** 1x Brass Sheet
- **Fluid Input:** 100 mB Lava
- **Output:** 1x Brass Ingot
- **Processing Time:** 100 ticks (5 seconds)

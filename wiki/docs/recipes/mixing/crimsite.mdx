
# Crimsite

This recipe allows you to produce Crimsite by mixing Cobblestone, Cinder Flour, and Redstone using Create's Mechanical Mixer.

## Recipe Details

| Input | Amount |
|---|---|
| ![Cobblestone](@minecraft:item/cobblestone){item} Cobblestone | 1 |
| ![Cinder Flour](@create:cinder_flour){item} Cinder Flour | 1 |
| ![Redstone](@minecraft:item/redstone){item} Redstone | 1 |

## Output

| Output | Amount |
|---|---|
| ![Crimsite](@create:crimsite){item} Crimsite | 1 |

## Processing Method

![Mechanical Mixer](@create:item/mechanical_mixer){item}

This recipe is processed using Create's Mechanical Mixer.

Place the required ingredients and fluids into a Basin and use the Mechanical Mixer to combine them into the resulting material.


## Processing Information

- **Machine:** Mechanical Mixer and Basin
- **Input:** 1x Cobblestone
- **Input:** 1x Cinder Flour
- **Input:** 1x Redstone
- **Output:** 1x Crimsite
- **Processing Time:** 100 ticks (5 seconds)

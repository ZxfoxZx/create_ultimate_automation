
# Crimsite

This recipe allows you to produce Crimsite by mixing Cobblestone, Cinder Flour, and Redstone using Create's Mechanical Mixer.

## Recipe Details

| Input | Amount |
|---|---|
| Cobblestone | 1 |
| Cinder Flour | 1 |
| Redstone | 1 |

## Output

| Output | Amount |
|---|---|
| Crimsite | 1 |

## Processing Information

- **Machine:** Mechanical Mixer and Basin
- **Input:** 1x Cobblestone
- **Input:** 1x Cinder Flour
- **Input:** 1x Redstone
- **Output:** 1x Crimsite
- **Processing Time:** 100 ticks (5 seconds)

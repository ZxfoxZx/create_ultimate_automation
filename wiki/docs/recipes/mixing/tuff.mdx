
# Tuff

This recipe allows you to produce Tuff by mixing Cobblestone, Cobbled Deepslate, Andesite, Diorite, and Granite using Create's Mechanical Mixer.

## Recipe Details

| Input | Amount |
|---|---|
| ![Cobblestone](@minecraft:item/cobblestone) Cobblestone | 1 |
| ![Cobbled Deepslate](@minecraft:item/cobbled_deepslate) Cobbled Deepslate | 1 |
| ![Andesite](@minecraft:item/andesite) Andesite | 1 |
| ![Diorite](@minecraft:item/diorite) Diorite | 1 |
| ![Granite](@minecraft:item/granite) Granite | 1 |

## Output

| Output | Amount |
|---|---|
| ![Tuff](@minecraft:item/tuff) Tuff | 16 |

## Processing Information

- **Machine:** Mechanical Mixer and Basin
- **Input:** 1x Cobblestone
- **Input:** 1x Cobbled Deepslate
- **Input:** 1x Andesite
- **Input:** 1x Diorite
- **Input:** 1x Granite
- **Output:** 16x Tuff
- **Processing Time:** 100 ticks (5 seconds)

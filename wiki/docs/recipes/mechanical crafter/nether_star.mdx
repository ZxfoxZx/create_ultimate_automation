
# Nether Star

This recipe allows you to craft a Nether Star using Create's Mechanical Crafters.

## Recipe Details

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Quartz | 6 |
| Wither Skeleton Skull | 3 |
| Soul Sand | 4 |
| Ender Pearl | 4 |
| Ender Eye | 1 |
| Netherite Ingot | 2 |

## Output

| Output | Amount |
|---|---|
| Nether Star | 1 |

## Processing Information

- **Machine:** Mechanical Crafters
- **Pattern Size:** 5x5
- **Quartz:** 6x
- **Wither Skeleton Skull:** 3x
- **Soul Sand:** 4x
- **Ender Pearl:** 4x
- **Ender Eye:** 1x
- **Netherite Ingot:** 2x
- **Output:** 1x Nether Star

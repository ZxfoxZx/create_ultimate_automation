
# Recipes & Processing

Welcome to the **Recipes & Processing** section of the Create: Ultimate Automation Wiki!

This section documents the additional recipes introduced by the mod, organized by their processing methods.

## Processing Categories

- **[Compacting](./compacting/)** - Recipes that use Create's Mechanical Press and Basin.
- **[Crushing](./crushing/)** - Recipes that use Create's Crushing Wheels.
- **[Cutting](./cutting/)** - Recipes that use Create's Mechanical Saw.
- **[Deploying](./deploying/)** - Recipes that use Create's Deployer.
- **[Mechanical Crafter](./mechanical_crafter/)** - Recipes that use Create's Mechanical Crafters.
- **[Milling](./milling/)** - Recipes that use Create's Millstone.
- **[Mixing](./mixing/)** - Recipes that use Create's Mechanical Mixer and Basin.
- **[Smelting](./smelting/)** - Recipes that use furnaces and other heat-based processing.

## Additional Mod Requirements

Some recipes require additional mods to be installed. These requirements are listed on their respective recipe pages.

More processing methods and recipes may be added in future updates.


# Pointed Dripstone

This recipe allows you to obtain Pointed Dripstone by cutting a Dripstone Block using Create's Mechanical Saw.

## Recipe Details

| Input | Amount |
|---|---|
| ![Dripstone Block](@minecraft:item/dripstone_block){item} Dripstone Block | 1 |

## Output

| Output | Amount |
|---|---|
| ![Pointed Dripstone](@minecraft:item/pointed_dripstone){item} Pointed Dripstone | 2 |

## Processing Information

- **Machine:** Mechanical Saw
- **Input:** 1x Dripstone Block
- **Output:** 2x Pointed Dripstone
- **Processing Time:** 100 ticks (5 seconds)

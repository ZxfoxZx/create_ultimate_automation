
# Emerald Ore

This recipe allows you to produce Emerald Ore by compacting Prismarine Shards, Amethyst Clusters, a Diamond, Sand, Cobblestone, and Lava using Create's Mechanical Press with heat.

## Recipe Details

| Input | Amount |
|---|---|
| ![Prismarine Shard](@minecraft:item/prismarine_shard) Prismarine Shard | 2 |
| ![Amethyst Cluster](@minecraft:item/amethyst_cluster) Amethyst Cluster | 2 |
| ![Diamond](@minecraft:item/diamond) Diamond | 1 |
| ![Sand](@minecraft:item/sand) Sand | 1 |
| ![Cobblestone](@minecraft:item/cobblestone) Cobblestone (Forge Tag: `forge:cobblestone`) | 1 |
| ![Lava](@minecraft:fluid/lava) Lava | 100 mB |

## Output

| Output | Amount |
|---|---|
| ![Emerald Ore](@minecraft:item/emerald_ore) Emerald Ore | 1 |

## Processing Information

- **Machine:** Mechanical Press and Basin
- **Input:** 2x Prismarine Shards
- **Input:** 2x Amethyst Clusters
- **Input:** 1x Diamond
- **Input:** 1x Sand
- **Input:** 1x Cobblestone (any block in the `forge:cobblestone` tag)
- **Fluid Input:** 100 mB Lava
- **Output:** 1x Emerald Ore
- **Heat Requirement:** Heated
- **Processing Time:** 200 ticks (10 seconds)

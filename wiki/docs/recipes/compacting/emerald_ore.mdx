
# Emerald Ore

This recipe allows you to produce Emerald Ore by compacting Prismarine Shards, Amethyst Clusters, a Diamond, Sand, Cobblestone, and Lava using Create's Mechanical Press with heat.

## Recipe Details

| Input | Amount |
|---|---|
| ![Prismarine Shard](@minecraft:item/prismarine_shard){item} Prismarine Shard | 2 |
| ![Amethyst Cluster](@minecraft:item/amethyst_cluster){item} Amethyst Cluster | 2 |
| ![Diamond](@minecraft:item/diamond){item} Diamond | 1 |
| ![Sand](@minecraft:item/sand){item} Sand | 1 |
| ![Cobblestone](@minecraft:item/cobblestone){item} Cobblestone (Forge Tag: `forge:cobblestone`) | 1 |
| ![Lava](@create_ultimate_automation:fluid/lava.gif){item} Lava | 100 mB |

## Output

| Output | Amount |
|---|---|
| ![Emerald Ore](@minecraft:item/emerald_ore){item} Emerald Ore | 1 |

## Processing Method

![Mechanical Press](@create:mechanical_press)

This recipe is processed using Create's Mechanical Press.

Place the required ingredients into a Basin and use the Mechanical Press to process them into the resulting material.


## Processing Information

- **Machine:** Mechanical Press and Basin
- **Input:** 2x Prismarine Shards
- **Input:** 2x Amethyst Clusters
- **Input:** 1x Diamond
- **Input:** 1x Sand
- **Input:** 1x Cobblestone (any block in the `forge:cobblestone` tag)
- **Fluid Input:** 100 mB Lava
- **Output:** 1x Emerald Ore
- **Heat Requirement:** Heated
- **Processing Time:** 200 ticks (10 seconds)


# Copper Ore

This recipe allows you to produce Copper Ore by compacting Cobblestone, Red Sand, and Redstone using Create's Mechanical Press with heat.

## Recipe Details

| Input | Amount |
|---|---|
| ![Cobblestone](@minecraft:item/cobblestone){item} Cobblestone (Forge Tag: `forge:cobblestone`) | 1 |
| ![Red Sand](@minecraft:item/red_sand){item} Red Sand | 2 |
| ![Redstone](@minecraft:item/redstone){item} Redstone | 2 |

## Output

| Output | Amount |
|---|---|
| ![Copper Ore](@minecraft:item/copper_ore){item} Copper Ore | 1 |


## Processing Method

![Mechanical Press](@create:mechanical_press)

This recipe is processed using Create's Mechanical Press.

Place the required ingredients into a Basin and use the Mechanical Press to process them into the resulting material.


## Processing Information

- **Machine:** Mechanical Press and Basin
- **Input:** 1x Cobblestone (any block in the `forge:cobblestone` tag)
- **Input:** 2x Red Sand
- **Input:** 2x Redstone
- **Output:** 1x Copper Ore
- **Heat Requirement:** Heated
- **Processing Time:** 100 ticks (5 seconds)

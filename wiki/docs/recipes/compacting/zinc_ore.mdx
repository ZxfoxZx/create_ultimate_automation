
# Zinc Ore

This recipe allows you to produce Zinc Ore by compacting Stone, Iron Ingots, and Redstone using Create's Mechanical Press with heat.

## Recipe Details

| Input | Amount |
|---|---|
| ![Stone](@minecraft:item/stone){item} Stone (Forge Tag: `forge:stone`) | 1 |
| ![Iron Ingot](@minecraft:item/iron_ingot){item} Iron Ingot | 2 |
| ![Redstone](@minecraft:item/redstone){item} Redstone | 2 |

## Output

| Output | Amount |
|---|---|
| ![Zinc Ore](@create:zinc_ore){item} Zinc Ore | 1 |

## Processing Method

![Mechanical Press](@create:item/mechanical_press)

This recipe is processed using Create's Mechanical Press.

Place the required ingredients into a Basin and use the Mechanical Press to process them into the resulting material.


## Processing Information

- **Machine:** Mechanical Press and Basin
- **Input:** 1x Stone (any block in the `forge:stone` tag)
- **Input:** 2x Iron Ingots
- **Input:** 2x Redstone
- **Output:** 1x Zinc Ore
- **Heat Requirement:** Heated
- **Processing Time:** 100 ticks (5 seconds)

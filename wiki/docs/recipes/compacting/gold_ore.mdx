
# Gold Ore

This recipe allows you to produce Gold Ore by compacting Cobblestone, Copper Ingots, Brass, and Blaze Powder using Create's Mechanical Press with heat.

## Recipe Details

| Input | Amount |
|---|---|
| ![Cobblestone](@minecraft:item/cobblestone){item} Cobblestone (Forge Tag: `forge:cobblestone`) | 1 |
| ![Copper Ingot](@minecraft:item/copper_ingot){item} Copper Ingot | 2 |
| ![Brass Ingot](@create:brass_ingot){item} Brass Ingot | 1 |
| ![Blaze Powder](@minecraft:item/blaze_powder){item} Blaze Powder | 2 |

## Output

| Output | Amount |
|---|---|
| ![Gold Ore](@minecraft:item/gold_ore){item} Gold Ore | 1 |

## Processing Method

![Mechanical Press](@create:item/mechanical_press)

This recipe is processed using Create's Mechanical Press.

Place the required ingredients into a Basin and use the Mechanical Press to process them into the resulting material.


## Processing Information

- **Machine:** Mechanical Press and Basin
- **Input:** 1x Cobblestone (any block in the `forge:cobblestone` tag)
- **Input:** 2x Copper Ingots
- **Input:** 1x Brass Ingot
- **Input:** 2x Blaze Powder
- **Output:** 1x Gold Ore
- **Heat Requirement:** Heated
- **Processing Time:** 100 ticks (5 seconds)


# Limestone

This recipe allows you to produce Limestone by compacting Cobblestone, Sand, and Gravel using Create's Mechanical Press.

## Recipe Details

| Input | Amount |
|---|---|
| ![Cobblestone](@minecraft:item/cobblestone){item} Cobblestone | 4 |
| ![Sand](@minecraft:item/sand){item} Sand | 2 |
| ![Gravel](@minecraft:item/gravel){item} Gravel | 1 |

## Output

| Output | Amount |
|---|---|
| ![Limestone](@create:limestone){item} Limestone | 1 |

## Processing Information

- **Machine:** Mechanical Press and Basin
- **Input:** 4x Cobblestone
- **Input:** 2x Sand
- **Input:** 1x Gravel
- **Output:** 1x Limestone
- **Processing Time:** 100 ticks (5 seconds)

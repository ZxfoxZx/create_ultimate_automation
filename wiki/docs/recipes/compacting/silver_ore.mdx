
# Silver Ore

This recipe allows you to produce Silver Ore by compacting Cobblestone, Iron Ingots, and Quartz using Create's Mechanical Press with heat.

## Recipe Details

| Input | Amount |
|---|---|
| ![Cobblestone](@minecraft:item/cobblestone) Cobblestone (Forge Tag: `forge:cobblestone`) | 1 |
| ![Iron Ingot](@minecraft:item/iron_ingot) Iron Ingot | 2 |
| ![Quartz](@minecraft:item/quartz) Quartz | 2 |

## Output

| Output | Amount |
|---|---|
| ![Silver Ore](@create_more_additions:silver_ore) Silver Ore | 1 |

## Processing Information

- **Machine:** Mechanical Press and Basin
- **Input:** 1x Cobblestone (any block in the `forge:cobblestone` tag)
- **Input:** 2x Iron Ingots
- **Input:** 2x Quartz
- **Output:** 1x Silver Ore
- **Heat Requirement:** Heated
- **Processing Time:** 100 ticks (5 seconds)
